# W19 region art direction

Task: `SR-20260909-150106-PPO12Y`
Milestone: W19 — Ash Railway + Eclipse Fortress route/world-connection expansion.

## Shared visual rules

- Preserve LANTERNFALL's dark maritime-fantasy readability hierarchy while moving the world inland and upward from W18.
- Region identity must be recognizable from silhouette and navigation grammar before color: converging rail geometry for Ash Railway, monumental occluding gates for Eclipse Fortress.
- Phase warnings, enemy telegraphs, circuit traces and Ark/player silhouettes remain visually dominant over scenery.
- W19 environment vectors are original repository-authored SVGs with no external image, texture, logo or font dependency.

## Ash Railway

Ash Railway is a broken logistics artery built across slag valleys. Parallel rails converge toward switch towers, signal lamps and a battered carriage silhouette. Material space emphasizes live switch lines and readable industrial geometry; shadow space turns trestles into missing spans and lets pursuit enemies approach through ghost-track gaps. Ember-gold accents are sparse navigation signals rather than a general color wash.

## Eclipse Fortress

Eclipse Fortress is a vertically dominant defensive complex arranged around a black-sun corona. Its grammar is repeated bastions, a nested central portcullis and long firing corridors that aim toward the player route. Material space uses lockbeam corridors and shield formations; shadow space uses occluded gates and crossfire pockets. The environment avoids copying the W13 shipyard by replacing horizontal dock silhouettes with monumental symmetric architecture.

## Persistent connection meaning

W19 uses the campaign's existing recoverable post-final choices as concrete world links rather than changing the W18 save schema. Ash Railway materializes `deep_rescue_patrol`; Eclipse Fortress materializes `lighthouse_survey`. Success persists their existing world-axis/access/unlock effects, while failure remains recoverable with a valid next departure. W21 owns the later five-region campaign-topology integration.

## Production boundary

These vectors are real runtime environment assets for W19 mechanics and presentation integration, but they are not the final commercial region-art acceptance set. Bosses, complete regional audio, animation/FX breadth, device readability and human art/balance review remain later gates, so W19 does not increment `implemented_content.regions` or `implemented_content.enemy_behaviors`.
