# W18 region art direction

Task: `SR-20260909-150106-PPO12Y`
Milestone: W18 — Glass Garden + Flooded Archive runtime-region expansion.

## Shared visual rules

- Preserve LANTERNFALL's dark maritime-fantasy base and the W13 lantern-gold player/Ark readability hierarchy.
- Region identity must come from silhouette, depth rhythm and navigation landmarks rather than recoloring the Twilight Shipyard.
- Gameplay warnings, phase overlays and circuit geometry remain higher-priority presentation layers than decorative environment detail.
- W18 environment vectors are authored repository source assets, not screenshots or externally sourced artwork.

## Glass Garden

The Glass Garden is a salt-fed greenhouse ruin where mineral growth has overtaken navigation infrastructure. Its dominant grammar is tall translucent prism blades, pale jade refraction lanes, lavender internal reflections and warm Ark light. Material-space landmarks read as solid prism corridors; shadow-space rules are represented in gameplay by mirror-root hazards and flanking pressure rather than by changing the base illustration alone.

## Flooded Archive

The Flooded Archive is a submerged records complex built from repeated vertical shelving/column masses. Its dominant grammar is deep blue-black depth, desaturated cyan water bands, sparse brass index marks and long horizontal current lines. Material-space hazards emphasize visible current lanes and archive columns; shadow-space hazards emphasize memory-undertow pockets and phase-aware enemies.

## Production boundary

These two vectors are real runtime environment assets for W18 mechanics and presentation integration. They are not the final region-art acceptance set: final animation polish, full prop/FX breadth, region-specific music/audio, bosses, device readability and human art-direction review remain later gates. Accordingly W18 must not increment `implemented_content.regions`.
