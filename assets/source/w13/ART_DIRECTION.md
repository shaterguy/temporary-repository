# W13 representative art direction

Milestone: W13 representative art
Region: Twilight Shipyard / 황혼 조선소

## Visual grammar

- Cold twilight steel and blue-violet distance fields frame a warm lantern-gold Ark and player focal light.
- Silhouettes must stay readable at combat scale: player coats point upward/outward, swarm variants use low or asymmetric mass, the boss uses a tall broken-navigator profile, and the Ark remains the widest warm shape.
- Shared contour language uses dark navy outlines, restrained metallic gradients, one warm emissive focal point per allied asset, and one hostile magenta/cyan signal per enemy family.
- Danger telegraphs use warm orange or phase violet rings and are drawn after decorative character art.
- Low-VFX mode may remove decorative pulse trails and secondary glows, but never danger rings, health information, Ark/objective state, or phase warnings.
