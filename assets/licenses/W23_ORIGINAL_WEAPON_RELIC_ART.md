# W23 original weapon and relic art provenance

Scope: `assets/runtime/w23/weapons/*.svg`, `assets/runtime/w23/relics/*.svg`
Milestone: W23B weapon/relic production art
Authorship date: 2026-09-10

The 18 weapon SVGs and 48 relic SVGs covered by this record were authored specifically for LANTERNFALL and committed as editable runtime vector sources. They do not embed stock art, third-party images, copied commercial-game assets, external fonts, icon packs, or separately licensed texture files.

Weapon identities are the exact 18 IDs returned by `game/data/weapon_part_catalog.gd`. Relic identities are the exact 48 IDs returned by `game/data/relic_catalog.gd` (8 families × 6 effect variants). Runtime and editable source are the same SVG files.

License/provenance status for this milestone: project-original source. Human visual-quality acceptance, balance acceptance, audio completion, and final release acceptance remain separate gates and are not implied by this provenance record.
