# R02 Rendered Runtime Load Soak

Status: `CANDIDATE_PENDING_CI`

## Scope

R02 is the post-R01 rendered-load and sustained-runtime gate for the current `v1.0.0-dev1` candidate. The test must use the real `game/ui/main_shell.tscn` runtime and production combat/presentation objects. Test-only stand-ins must not be counted as enemies, projectile delivery, or decorative effects.

The required workload is:

- at least 600 simultaneous production `EnemyPool` entities;
- at least 1000 production weapon deliveries through the real causal weapon resolver and `weapon_action` signal path;
- at least 300 simultaneous decorative VFX entries in the production representative-art queue;
- 300 rendered frames after the load is established;
- a successful persistent checkpoint after the sustained load;
- a rendered 1280x720 evidence image and recorded host frame/memory telemetry.

## Projectile-load semantics

The current production architecture has no projectile node, projectile scene, or projectile state model. A weapon trigger resolves immediately to one or more `weapon_damage` actions in `causal_weapon_model.gd`; `survivor_controller.gd` applies those actions and emits the production `weapon_action` signal, which the representative art layer consumes for impact VFX.

Accordingly, R02 does not invent 1000 test-only projectile nodes merely to satisfy a number. `R02_PROJECTILE_LOAD_TARGET=1000` means at least 1000 observed production `weapon_action` deliveries generated by `_resolve_events()` against real active targets. The log must also emit `R02_PROJECTILE_NODE_MODEL=ABSENT_BY_DESIGN` and `R02_PROJECTILE_LOAD_SEMANTICS=weapon_action` so this architecture-specific interpretation cannot be mistaken for a physical-projectile implementation.

## Rendered workload construction

`tests/integration/r02_rendered_load_soak.gd`:

1. boots the actual main shell with the real W22 campaign runtime and enters an expedition through the public slot/world-choice flow;
2. adds 600 high-health, zero-contact entries through the production `EnemyPool.acquire()` implementation and binds them to the currently targetable phase;
3. drives the production combat resolver until at least 1000 real `weapon_action` signals have been observed;
4. verifies that those actions populate the production representative-art `_bursts` queue rather than a test-owned effect collection;
5. lets the first burst expire through the production VFX lifetime logic;
6. rebuilds at least 300 real impact VFX entries through the same production resolver, freezes only VFX aging for 30 rendered frames to hold a stable simultaneous render load, and then restores normal aging;
7. renders a total of 300 sustained frames while retaining at least 600 active enemies;
8. checkpoints the loaded expedition and records host frame percentiles and static-memory observations;
9. writes `artifacts/r02/peak-600-1000-300-1280x720.png` from the actual SubViewport.

The VFX-aging freeze is a load-harness control only. It does not replace the production renderer or create synthetic presentation objects; it keeps already-created production impact effects alive long enough to measure a fixed concurrent load.
