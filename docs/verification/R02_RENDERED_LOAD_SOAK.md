# R02 Rendered Runtime Load Soak

Status: `ACTIVE_CI_GATE`

## Scope

R02 is the post-R01 rendered-load and sustained-runtime gate for the current `v1.0.0-dev1` candidate. The test must use the real `game/ui/main_shell.tscn` runtime and production combat/presentation objects. Test-only stand-ins must not be counted as enemies, projectile delivery, or decorative effects.

The required workload is:

- at least 600 simultaneous production `EnemyPool` entities;
- at least 1000 production weapon deliveries through the real causal weapon resolver and `weapon_action` signal path;
- at least 300 simultaneous decorative VFX entries in the production representative-art queue;
- 300 rendered frames after the load is established;
- a successful persistent checkpoint after the sustained load;
- a rendered 1280x720 evidence image and recorded host frame/memory telemetry.

## Projectile-load semantics

The current production architecture has no projectile node, projectile scene, or projectile state model. A weapon trigger resolves immediately to one or more `weapon_damage` actions in `causal_weapon_model.gd`; `survivor_controller.gd` applies those actions and emits the production `weapon_action` signal, which the representative art layer consumes for impact VFX.

Accordingly, R02 does not invent 1000 test-only projectile nodes merely to satisfy a number. `R02_PROJECTILE_LOAD_TARGET=1000` means at least 1000 observed production `weapon_action` deliveries generated by `_resolve_events()` against real active targets. The log also emits `R02_PROJECTILE_NODE_MODEL=ABSENT_BY_DESIGN` and `R02_PROJECTILE_LOAD_SEMANTICS=weapon_action` so this architecture-specific interpretation cannot be mistaken for a physical-projectile implementation.

## Rendered workload construction

`tests/integration/r02_rendered_load_soak.gd`:

1. boots the actual main shell with the real W22 campaign runtime and enters an expedition through the public slot/world-choice flow;
2. verifies a baseline production checkpoint before applying the heavy load;
3. freezes only `MainShell._process()` during the host-render measurement so llvmpipe wall-clock slowness cannot independently trigger the shell's 15-second periodic checkpoint or deferred death settlement; child production runtimes and rendering remain active;
4. adds 600 high-health, zero-contact entries through the production `EnemyPool.acquire()` implementation and binds them to the currently targetable phase;
5. pauses automatic player attacks while driving the production combat resolver until at least 1000 real `weapon_action` signals have been observed;
6. verifies that those actions populate the production representative-art `_bursts` queue rather than a test-owned effect collection;
7. advances the production VFX lifetime routine directly by a value greater than the production `BURST_LIFETIME`, instead of waiting 90 slow host-render frames that can distort unrelated wall-clock policy;
8. rebuilds at least 300 real impact VFX entries through the same production resolver, freezes only VFX aging for 30 rendered frames to hold a stable simultaneous render load, and then restores normal VFX aging;
9. renders a total of 300 sustained frames while retaining at least 600 active enemies;
10. checkpoints the loaded expedition, restores normal shell/combat processing, and records host frame percentiles and static-memory observations;
11. writes `artifacts/r02/peak-600-1000-300-1280x720.png` from the actual SubViewport.

The harness controls do not replace the production renderer, combat resolver, EnemyPool, save path, or VFX objects. They isolate frame-load measurement from CI-host wall-clock policy so the test measures the declared workload rather than the speed of llvmpipe.

## Result recording rule

The CI run attached to the exact commit is the authoritative pass/fail record.
